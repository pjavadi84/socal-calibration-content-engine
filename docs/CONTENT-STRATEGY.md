## 1. Strategy Overview

### Goals

- Rank for high-intent local calibration keywords across Southern California
- Build topical authority in calibration, compliance, and metrology
- Drive organic traffic that converts into calibration service inquiries
- Ramp content velocity safely to avoid scaled-content-abuse detection

| Months 1–2    | 12                 | Establish baseline, build trust        |

2. **Industry Compliance** — authority-building, decision-maker audience

## 6. Monthly Content Mix

### Recommended Ratio

| Content Type                      | % of Monthly Output | Location? | Purpose                    |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------- | -------------------------- |
| Service + Location pages          | 60%                 | Yes       | Revenue-driving, local SEO |
| Compliance & Standards articles   | 25%                 | No        | Authority-building         |
| Equipment Guides / Industry Apps  | 15%                 | Mixed     | Top-of-funnel traffic      |

### Why This Mix Works

- **Service + location pages** target people searching "[service] in [city]" — these are bottom-of-funnel, ready to buy
- **Compliance articles** rank for informational queries and build E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust) signals that Google uses to rank your entire domain higher
- **Equipment guides** attract early-stage researchers who may need calibration later, plus they create natural internal linking opportunities to service pages

## 7. Cluster Linking Strategy

### What Is a Content Cluster?

A content cluster groups related articles that link to each other, signaling to Google that your site has deep expertise on a topic.

### Cluster Structure

```
[Pillar: Calibration Services]
  └─ [Category: Scale Calibration]
       ├── Scale Calibration in Irvine (links to → LA, San Diego, general)
       ├── Scale Calibration in Los Angeles (links to → Irvine, Long Beach)
       ├── Scale Calibration in San Diego (links to → Irvine, LA)
       └── Scale Calibration in Long Beach (links to → LA, Anaheim)

[Pillar: Industry Compliance]
  └─ [Category: NIST Traceability]
       └── What Is NIST Traceability (links to → all scale/electrical/pressure calibration articles)

[Pillar: Equipment Guides]
  └─ [Category: Scales & Balances]
       └── Industrial Scales Guide (links to → all scale calibration location articles)
```

### Rules

1. **Generate 3–4 articles in the same category before moving on** — this builds the cluster
2. **Compliance and equipment articles link DOWN to service pages** — passes authority to revenue pages
3. **Service pages link ACROSS to sibling locations** — builds geographic cluster
4. The content engine's `injectClusterLinks` function handles this automatically when sibling articles exist

1. **Generate** article via content engine (auto-pushed as WordPress draft)
2. **Review** draft in WordPress (check facts, tone, images)
3. **Publish** — aim to publish within 24–48 hours of generation

- Publish **3 articles per week** (Mon/Wed/Fri)

### Monthly Review Checklist

- [ ] Check top 10 performing articles by traffic
- [ ] Identify articles ranking on page 2 (positions 11–20) — candidates for optimization
- [ ] Review Yoast SEO scores for newly published articles
- [ ] Verify featured images display correctly on all new posts
- [ ] Check internal link clusters are forming (received/outgoing links in Yoast)
- [ ] Monitor content velocity — stay within safe limits

| Tier       | Criteria                    | Action                          |
